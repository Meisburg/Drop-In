V6 batch 2: type up to 17px body + real text contrast

"Still feels small" after the 16px pass, so the floor moves to iOS's own body
size: 17px (was 14 two steps ago). Headings 26px, meta 14px, brand 32px. The
audit floor rises to match — nothing under 14px passes.

The bigger cause of "small" was probably CONTRAST, not size: text-slate-500 was
used 81 times and text-slate-400 32 times. slate-400 on white is about 2.9:1 —
under the 4.5:1 WCAG AA floor — so a lot of the app's meta text was genuinely
hard to read at any size. Both tiers shift up one (400->500, 500->600) and the
hierarchy is preserved rather than flattened: 93 secondary, 32 tertiary, and
zero failing-contrast grays left.

Measured after: 17px body / 26px heading, 12 viewport-route combinations green,
290/290 unit.
