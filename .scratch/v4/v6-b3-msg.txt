Two more items from the first phone test.

1. The going toggle was a bare circle in the card's badge cluster, and it read
   as a status badge: "it's not clear that that's indicating that you're
   going". It is now a labelled pill in the card's action row — "I'm going"

   context because photos live in five different components). Tap to open,
   tap anywhere or press Escape to close, body scroll locked, 44px close.
   Decision #3 applied: on the detail page the avatar and the name now do
   different things — tapping the PHOTO enlarges it, the NAME goes to the
   profile. They used to be one link, which is exactly why the tap did the
   unexpected thing. Kid photos on a profile expand too.
